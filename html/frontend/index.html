Why People Choose PayBee

Built on absolute security, transparency, and accountability.

Every Account Is Verified

No anonymous users. Every buyer and seller on PayBee has been identity-checked and approved by our admin team before their first trade.

USDT Stays in Escrow

The moment a trade begins, the seller's USDT is locked. It cannot be moved, withdrawn, or touched until both parties complete the trade correctly.

Payment Proof Required

Before any USDT is released, the buyer uploads a screenshot of their bank transfer. The seller reviews it. Only after manual confirmation does the ledger update.

Real-Time Trade Tracking

Every trade has a live status, a countdown timer, and a full action log. You always know exactly where things stand.

Zero Counterparty Risk

PayBee's escrow and admin oversight model means there is no scenario where you pay and receive nothing, or release USDT and receive nothing.

Three Networks Supported

Deposit and withdraw USDT on TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20. All three networks are verified directly on-chain before your balance is credited.
