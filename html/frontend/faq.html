Getting Started

How do I create a PayBee account?

Fill in your registration details on the website or app and submit your identity documents for KYC verification. Our admin team reviews your submission and activates your account manually. You'll receive your login credentials once approved. This is not an instant self-signup — admin activation is required for every account.

Why does PayBee require admin account activation?

We verify every user before they can trade. This is how we ensure that every buyer and seller on the platform is a real, verified individual. It eliminates anonymous fraud from the start.

How long does account activation take?

Most accounts are activated within a few hours during business hours. If your documents are clear and complete, the process is straightforward.

Which networks are supported for USDT?

PayBee currently supports TRC20, ERC20, and BEP20. Make sure you deposit using the correct network — deposits sent on an unsupported network cannot be recovered.

Deposits

How do I deposit USDT?

After your account is activated, you'll see your assigned PayBee wallet address in the dashboard. Send USDT from your external wallet to that address, then submit the transaction ID (TXID) on the platform.

What happens after I submit my TXID?

Our admin team queries the blockchain explorer using your TXID. We confirm the amount, the network, and the number of confirmations. Once all checks pass, your balance is credited. You'll receive a notification when this is complete.

How long does deposit verification take?

Verification is typically completed within 15 minutes. If your deposit has been pending longer than that, contact support with your TXID and network details.

What if my TXID is wrong or the amount doesn't match?

If the TXID is invalid or the transferred amount doesn't match what was expected, the deposit will be marked as failed. Our admin team will investigate and contact you. Do not re-send until you've spoken with support.

Trading

What is escrow and why does it protect me?

Escrow means the seller's USDT is moved into a neutral locked account at the start of every trade. It cannot be withdrawn by the seller or transferred elsewhere while the trade is active. You only send your bank transfer after escrow is confirmed. The USDT only releases to you after the seller confirms receipt of your payment. At no point is either party unprotected.

How long do I have to complete my bank transfer?

Each sell ad has a payment window set by the seller — typically 30 minutes. The timer is visible on your trade screen. If the timer runs out before you upload proof, the trade is automatically cancelled and the USDT is returned to the seller.

What proof do I need to upload?

A screenshot of your completed bank transfer. It should clearly show the sender name, recipient details, amount, date, and a transaction reference. Edited or metadata-tampered files are rejected by the system.

What happens if the seller doesn't confirm my payment?

If the seller is unresponsive for more than 24 hours after you upload valid proof, you can raise a dispute. Our admin team will review the evidence, attempt to contact the seller, and issue a force-release of the escrowed USDT to you if your proof is valid.

Selling

How do I post a sell ad?

Once your USDT is deposited and your balance is credited, go to the P2P Marketplace and create a sell ad. Set your amount, exchange rate, minimum and maximum trade size, and which banks you accept. Your USDT locks into escrow the moment the ad goes live.

Can I pause or cancel my sell ad?

You can pause an active ad at any time if there are no open trades on it. If a trade is already in progress, you cannot cancel until the trade is resolved.

What if a buyer's screenshot doesn't match my bank statement?

Do not confirm. Raise a dispute immediately. Our admin team will review both the uploaded screenshot and your bank records, then make a fair determination. Never release USDT if you have not verified receipt in your actual bank account.

Disputes

How do I raise a dispute?

On the active trade page, click the Dispute button. Describe the issue and submit any supporting evidence — bank statements, screenshots, communication records. Our team will review and respond within 24 hours in most cases.

Can admin override my escrow?

In dispute situations, admin can force-release USDT to the buyer or force-refund it to the seller based on the evidence. Every override requires a written reason that is permanently recorded in the audit trail.

Security

Is my personal data safe?

Your personal information is encrypted at rest and in transit. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. It is used only for identity verification, trade processing, and regulatory compliance.

Do I need two-factor authentication?

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA on your account. It is required for all admin accounts. You can enable it for login, withdrawals, and trade confirmations in your account settings.
