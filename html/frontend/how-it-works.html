STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How PayBee Works

Whether you're buying or selling USDT, the process is clear, protected, and simple to follow.

1

Submit Your Registration

Fill in your details and upload your identity documents. Our admin team reviews and activates your account manually. You'll receive your login credentials once approved.

2

Deposit USDT to Your Wallet

Send USDT to your assigned PayBee wallet address on TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20.

3

Submit Your Transaction ID

Once sent, paste your blockchain transaction ID (TXID) into the platform dashboard.

4

Admin Verifies On-Chain

Our admin team queries the blockchain explorer directly, confirms the amount, network, and confirmation count against your TXID.

5

Balance Credited to Your Account

Once verification is complete, your USDT balance is credited to your PayBee wallet. You'll receive a notification when this happens and can see it reflected in your dashboard immediately.

6

Browse Available Sell Offers

View active sell offers on the P2P marketplace. Filter by rate, amount range, and accepted banks. Pick the one that suits you.

7

Initiate a Trade

Click Trade on a sell offer. The platform immediately locks the seller's USDT in escrow. The seller's bank details are revealed to you — and only you — once escrow is confirmed.

8

Send the Bank Transfer

Transfer the fiat amount to the seller's bank account using your online banking.

9

Upload Your Payment Proof

Screenshot your completed bank transfer and upload it to the trade page. The system hashes and timestamps your file the moment it is received.

10

Seller Reviews and Confirms

The seller checks your screenshot against their bank records. Once satisfied, they confirm payment received on the platform.

11

USDT Released to Your Wallet

The escrowed USDT is released to your PayBee wallet immediately after seller confirmation. The trade is marked complete and archived with a full audit trail.

1

Account Created by Admin

Contact PayBee to open a seller account. Our admin team creates your account, assigns your role, and activates your wallet and trading permissions.

2

Deposit Your USDT

Send USDT to your assigned PayBee wallet address on TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20. Submit your TXID on the dashboard once sent.

3

Admin Verifies Your Deposit

Admin queries the blockchain explorer, confirms your transaction on-chain, and credits your balance. You are notified once your wallet is funded and ready.

4

Buyer Initiates a Trade With You

When a buyer on the platform selects you for a trade, the platform automatically locks the relevant USDT amount from your wallet into hard-locked escrow. You receive a trade notification with a countdown timer.

5

Your Bank Details Are Shared Securely

The platform transmits your bank details to the matched buyer only — encrypted and restricted to that trade session. No one else can see them.

6

Buyer Sends the Bank Transfer

The buyer has a set payment window to complete their bank transfer and upload a screenshot of the transaction as proof.

7

Review the Payment Proof

You receive a notification when the buyer uploads their proof. Review the screenshot carefully against your actual bank statement. Take your time — do not confirm until you have verified the transfer in your account.

8

Confirm or Dispute

If the payment matches — confirm receipt inside the platform. This action requires your PIN or 2FA token to prevent accidental releases. If something is wrong — raise a dispute immediately and admin will step in.

9

Trade Closed, Funds Settled

Once you confirm, the escrowed USDT is released to the buyer. Your bank holds the fiat. Your ledger is updated with any applicable fee deduction. The trade is archived with a full audit trail.

Disputes

If you cannot resolve a trade — because payment wasn't sent, the screenshot doesn't match, or either party is unresponsive — raise a dispute directly on the trade page. Our admin team reviews the evidence submitted by both sides, contacts the relevant parties if needed, and issues a force-release or force-refund based on what the proof shows. Most disputes are resolved within 24 hours.

PayBee is a peer-to-peer exchange platform where you convert USDT into local currency and receive it straight to your bank account. Secure, transparent, and protected by our Escrow Engine.
