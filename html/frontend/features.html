PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

What's Inside PayBee

Every feature on PayBee exists to make your USDT-to-bank trades faster, safer, and more transparent.

For Buyers

Zero Platform Fee

Buyers pay no platform fee on completed trades. What you see in the ad is what you pay.

Verified Seller Ads Only

Every seller on the platform has been admin-verified. You're never trading with an anonymous account.

Escrow on Every Trade

As soon as you initiate a trade, the seller's USDT is locked. It cannot be withdrawn or moved until settled correctly.

Encrypted Bank Details

The seller's bank account details are only revealed to you after escrow is confirmed — and only to you.

30-Min Payment Window

You have a clear, timed window to complete your bank transfer and upload proof. The timer is visible so there's no confusion.

Instant Wallet Credit

Once the seller confirms your payment, USDT arrives in your PayBee wallet immediately. No processing delay.

For Sellers

Set Your Own Rate

Post your sell ad at the exchange rate you want. You control the price, trade size, and accepted banks.

Hard Lock Protection

Your USDT moves into escrow the moment your ad is live. Your funds are always accounted for and never double-spent.

Screenshot Confirmation

You only release USDT after physically reviewing the buyer's bank transfer. Confirmation requires PIN/2FA to prevent accidents.

Earn on Every Trade

Sellers earn profit on each completed trade through their own exchange rate spread. Active sellers qualify for reduced fee tiers.

Dispute Protection

If a buyer's screenshot doesn't match your bank statement, raise a dispute. Admin holds the escrow until a fair resolution is reached.

Full Trade History

Every completed and cancelled trade is recorded in your account with full details, timestamps, and downloadable records.

Three Network Support

Deposit and withdraw USDT on TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20. Verified independently on-chain.

On-Chain Verification

We query the blockchain explorer for your TXID, check the amount, network, and confirmations. No trust assumptions.

Double-Entry Ledger

Every transaction creates a matched debit and credit record, linked cryptographically. Balances are tamper-evident.

Permanent Audit Trail

Every event — deposit, trade, dispute — is logged with a timestamp, actor ID, and reason. Nothing is deleted.

Two-Factor Authentication

Protect your account with TOTP-based 2FA on login, withdrawals, and trade confirmations. Strongly recommended.

24/7 Live Support

Our support team is available around the clock for trade help, deposit questions, and dispute guidance.

PayBee is a peer-to-peer exchange platform where you convert USDT into local currency and receive it straight to your bank account. Secure, transparent, and protected by our Escrow Engine.
