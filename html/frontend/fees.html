Network & Transfer Fees

Deposits

Depositing USDT to PayBee is free on our side.

Standard network fees apply from your external wallet to the blockchain, as determined by the relevant network (TRC20, ERC20, BEP20) at the time of transfer. PayBee does not charge a deposit fee.

Withdrawals

Withdrawing USDT incurs a network fee based on current blockchain conditions. We pass this fee through at cost — we do not mark it up.

Sellers withdrawing fiat proceeds to their bank accounts go through an admin-approved process. Processing fees, if any, are shown at the time of the request.
