About PayBee

Connecting buyers and sellers through an unbreakable ledger of trust.

What PayBee Is

PayBee is a peer-to-peer USDT-to-bank exchange platform. We connect people who want to buy USDT with people who want to sell it — and we make sure every single trade is protected, traceable, and settled fairly.

The core idea is straightforward. If you hold USDT and want local currency in your bank account, PayBee connects you with a verified buyer. If you want to buy USDT by sending a bank transfer, PayBee connects you with a verified seller. In both cases, the USDT is held in escrow, payment proof is uploaded and reviewed, and nothing moves until both sides are satisfied.

Why We Built It

Converting USDT to local currency used to mean trusting strangers on chat groups, wiring money with no protection, and hoping the other side would follow through. It was slow, risky, and had no accountability.

We built PayBee to fix that. Every trade on our platform is admin-supervised, escrow-protected, and logged with a permanent audit trail. If something goes wrong, there is a dispute process with real human review and a resolution. Nothing is left to chance.

How We Operate

PayBee runs on an admin-governed model. Every action is meticulously verified and recorded.

1

Admin Verification

Every account is created and verified by our team before trading can begin.

2

On-Chain Checks

We query the blockchain explorer directly for confirmation count before crediting any balance.

3

Hard-Locked Escrow

Every trade is held in a hard-locked escrow that only releases on confirmed evidence.

Double-Entry Ledger

Cryptographically chained. Any attempt to alter past records is immediately detectable.
